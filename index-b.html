How it works

Three steps. One winner a month. No fine print.

1

Join the pool

Free signup. Your name goes into every drawing until you win. Have debt or just want to help — everyone's welcome.

2

We crowdfund together

Members and allies chip in what they can — $5, $50, whatever works. 100% goes directly to a loan servicer, not to us.

3

Someone gets free

We draw a name, verify their debt, and wire the funds straight to their lender. Then we do it again next month.
