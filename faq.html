Frequently asked questions

The honest answers — including the awkward ones.

We love the Debt Collective. They're doing essential political organizing — pressuring Congress, the Department of Education, and lenders to cancel debt at scale. Their work has helped cancel nearly $200 billion in loans. Go support them too. But political wins take years, and millions of people are drowning right now. We're a mutual aid pool, not a movement. We pay off real debt this month, with real money, for one real person. Different lane, same fight.

Honestly? Low at first, and we won't pretend otherwise. With 50 members in the pool, you've got a 1-in-50 shot at the first drawing. As we grow, individual odds go down — but the number of payoffs per year goes up. Most members will never win. Most will still tell you it was worth joining, because someone they'll never meet got their life back, and that someone could just as easily have been them.

In a dedicated account we don't touch for anything else. We publish the balance on our homepage in real time. When a drawing happens, the entire balance (minus credit card processing fees, which are unavoidable at roughly 2.9% + $0.30 per donation) goes directly to the recipient's loan servicer. We pay the lender, not the recipient — so there's no ambiguity about where the money went. Annual transparency report coming once we have a year of data.

No salaries, no overhead skim. The only money that doesn't reach a recipient is what credit card processors charge and a small hosting bill for the website. We publish those numbers. If we ever need to pay someone to keep this running, we'll tell you before we do it.

This is the awkward one. Under current IRS rules, gifts above the annual exclusion can have tax implications, and debt paid on someone's behalf can be treated as income in some structures. We're working with a CPA to set this up the right way — likely as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit so payoffs qualify as charitable gifts. Until that's finalized, we'll be transparent with each recipient about their tax exposure, and we'll cover any tax owed as part of the payoff. You don't get rescued from one debt just to land in another.

Yes, and we need you. Members in the pool are eligible to be drawn. Allies fund the pool. Both roles matter. If you've already paid off your loans — or never had them — you can donate without signing up for drawings. Every dollar goes to someone who's still buried.

Right now, our drawings will likely cover up to the pool's current balance. If we draw someone with $200,000 in loans and we've raised $15,000, we'll pay $15,000 toward their balance and roll them back into the pool for future drawings. We'd rather make a real dent than turn anyone away.
