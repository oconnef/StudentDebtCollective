The honest answers — including the awkward ones.

We love the Debt Collective. They're doing essential political organizing — pressuring Congress, the Department of Education, and lenders to cancel debt at scale. Their work has helped cancel nearly $200 billion in loans. Go support them too. But political wins take years, and millions of people are drowning right now. We're a mutual aid pool, not a movement. We pay off real debt this month, with real money, for one real person. Different lane, same fight.

Honestly? Low at first, and we won't pretend otherwise. With 100 members in the drawing, you've got a 1-in-100 shot at the first drawing. As we grow, individual odds go down — but the number of payoffs per year goes up. Most members will never win. Most will still tell you it was worth joining, because someone they'll never meet got their life back, and that someone could just as easily have been them.

Nowhere — and that's the point. We never hold money. Here's the exact sequence: we draw a name → we set up a GoFundMe for that person → we blast it to our entire community → GoFundMe collects the donations → at the end of the drive, GoFundMe sends the funds directly to the recipient's loan servicer. The money goes from your card to GoFundMe to the lender. It never passes through our hands at all. GoFundMe does charge a platform fee (currently around 2.9% + $0.30 per donation), which is unavoidable — but that's it.

No. We don't collect money, so there's nothing to skim. GoFundMe charges their standard processing fee on each donation (around 2.9% + $0.30) — that goes to them, not us. Our only costs are the website hosting and the email platform, which we cover ourselves. If that ever changes, we'll say so publicly before it does.

This is the awkward one. Under current IRS rules, gifts above the annual exclusion can have tax implications, and debt paid on someone's behalf can be treated as income in some structures. We're working with a CPA to set this up the right way — likely as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit so payoffs qualify as charitable gifts. Until that's finalized, we'll be transparent with each recipient about their tax exposure, and we'll cover any tax owed as part of the payoff. You don't get rescued from one debt just to land in another.

Yes, and we need you. Members in the drawing are eligible to win. Allies donate when each GoFundMe goes live. Both roles matter. If you've already paid off your loans — or never had them — you can follow us, share each fundraiser, and donate when a drive opens. Every dollar goes to someone who's still buried.

The GoFundMe runs for a set period and whatever gets raised goes to the winner's servicer. If someone has $200,000 in loans and the drive raises $8,000, we send $8,000 and roll them back into the drawing for future rounds. A meaningful dent is better than nothing, and they stay eligible until they're paid off.
